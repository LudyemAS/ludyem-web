Good to know
Where is my data?+

All of it stays on your phone: your plan, your logs, your cravings, your reasons and your photo. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it isn't synced to iCloud. The privacy policy has the detail.

How do I export or delete my data?+

In Lungday's Settings you can export your logs as a CSV file, or delete everything, at any time.

How do I restore Lungday Pro?+

Open Settings in Lungday and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Is Lungday medical advice?+

No. Lungday supports your quit attempt. It isn't medical advice. If you are thinking about varenicline, nicotine replacement or cytisine, ask your doctor or pharmacist which is right for you.
