How it works

The target moves with you.

You tap to say you did it. That is the whole interaction. Everything else follows from the days you actually showed up.

The ladder

A ladder, not a checkbox.

Every quest starts at a target you can hit, measured from your own last fourteen days. Hit it four days out of five and it rises a step. Miss it and it comes back down, quietly, with nothing counted against you.

A character made of what you did.

Six stats, a level and a rank, and not one number rises without a habit behind it. Your character sheet is a reading of your month, not a score.

Today

Your quests for the day, morning to evening. Apple Health ticks steps, exercise minutes and sleep for you, so a walk you took with the app closed is already done when you open it.

  • HistoryEvery past day, a month at a time, quest by quest.

Privacy

Your habits stay on your iPhone.

No account, no ads, and no Health data going anywhere. The app counts a few anonymous moments, like a quest ticked, by name only: never what the quest is, its target or your stats.
