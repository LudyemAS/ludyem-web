How it works

The target moves with you

You tap to say you did it. That is the whole interaction. Everything below follows from the days you actually showed up.

🪜

A ladder, not a checkbox

Hit your target on 4 of your last 5 scheduled days and it rises. Miss it on 3 of 5 and it eases back, never below where you started.

🎓

Every habit has a top

Hold the ceiling and the habit graduates. It stops climbing and stays there. A ladder with no top is a treadmill.

❄️

Freezes for the bad weeks

Spend a freeze and the day goes neutral. Your streak survives. Being ill should not wipe out ninety days of work.

🍎

Apple Health ticks it for you

Build a habit on steps, exercise minutes or sleep and it marks itself done. Read only, never written, never leaves your iPhone.

⬆️

A level, six stats, a rank

Strength, Vitality, Intellect, Focus, Spirit and Order. Every one of them comes from a habit you actually did. Nothing here can be bought.

🔒

Nothing leaves the phone

No account, no backend, no analytics, no ads. There is nothing to breach because there is nothing to hold.

Privacy

On your iPhone. Full stop.

No account, no sign-in, no tracking, and no Health data going anywhere. Read the detail rather than taking our word for it.
