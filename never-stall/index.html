How it works

The target moves with you

You tap to say you did it. That is the whole interaction. Everything below follows from the days you actually showed up.

A ladder, not a checkbox

Hit your target on 4 of your last 5 scheduled days and it rises. Miss it on 3 of 5 and it eases back, never below where you started.

Every habit has a top

Hold the ceiling and the habit graduates. It stops climbing and stays there, with a seal on its page. A ladder with no top is a treadmill.

Freezes for the bad weeks

Spend a freeze and the day goes neutral. Your streak survives and the ladder holds. Being ill should not wipe out ninety days of work.

Apple Health ticks it for you

Build a habit on steps, exercise minutes or sleep and it marks itself done. Read only, never written, and it never leaves your iPhone.

A level, six stats, a rank

Every one of them comes from a habit you actually did, and the rank runs E to S. Nothing on this screen can be bought.
