Inside every app

A real trainer. Not just a counter.

The same engine powers all four, so whichever you pick, you get the whole thing.

A plan that adapts to you.

Tell it your current max and your goal. It builds a multi-week ladder of sets and rest that ramps you there, and a max test recalibrates it whenever you get stronger.

Every rep counted.

One tap per rep, or hands-free: count out loud, or let the front camera count your push-ups and sit-ups. A rest timer tells you when to go again.

Nine ranks to climb.

From Beginner to Legend, with the goal in the app's name as the summit. Your best single set is what promotes you.

Your progress, all of it.

A weekly streak worth protecting, a calendar of every session, and month, year and all-time stats. Every set is saved to Apple Health, so your effort counts toward your rings.

  • Apple HealthEvery session is written to Health as a workout.
  • Widgets and Live ActivitiesToday's sets on your Home Screen, the set in progress on your Lock Screen.
  • BadgesEarned for the sessions, streaks and records behind them.
  • No gym, no gearJust your bodyweight and a few minutes a day.
  • No accountOpen the app and train. Nothing to sign up for.
  • Eleven languagesEnglish, plus German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Dutch, Portuguese and Chinese.

Privacy

On your iPhone. Full stop.

No account, no sign-in, no tracking. Camera counting runs entirely on your iPhone: frames are analysed and discarded, never saved or uploaded.
