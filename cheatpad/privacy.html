Privacy Policy

Cheatpad collects nothing about you.

Last updated 4 October 2026 · Applies to Cheatpad by Ludyem AS.

Cheatpad has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you choose in the app stays on your phone: your platform, your favorite codes, your checklist ticks, your read-aloud pace and whether reminders are on.

Code updates

So that new codes arrive without an app update, Cheatpad downloads the latest code list from our server, which is hosted by Cloudflare. The request carries nothing about you. Like any web request it comes from your IP address, which Cloudflare uses to deliver the file and protect its network. We don't use it to identify you.

Cloudflare's privacy policy

Reminders

Launch reminders are scheduled on the phone itself, and only if you turn them on. Nothing is sent from a server.

Reporting a code

If you tap Report this code, your Mail app opens a message to us with the code, your platform and the version of the code list. You decide whether to send it. We use it only to check the code and to reply to you.

Not affiliated with Rockstar Games

Cheatpad is an independent, unofficial guide. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto is a trademark of Take-Two Interactive.
