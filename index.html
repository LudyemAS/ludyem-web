Our promise

Eleven apps. One set of rules.

Not features we switch on per app. It is how everything here is built.

  • No ads. Ever.Not in a free tier and not in a paid one. None of our apps carries an ad or a tracking SDK.
  • Your data is not for sale.We never sell or share what you put into our apps, and most of it never reaches us at all.
  • On your device first.Health and money apps keep your data on your iPhone, in Apple Health or in your own iCloud.
  • No account unless the job needs one.Open the app and it works. The developer tools sign in only to the services they read for you.
  • Made for Apple, all the way down.Native apps with widgets, Siri, Apple Health and Apple Watch where they make sense.
  • A person answers.Write to support@ludyem.dev and a person reads every email, whichever app it is about.
