Features

A diary that gives back. Then lets go.

Most trackers keep your data. Tend logs fast, finds what moves you, and hands everything to Apple Health.

Apple Health

Your record outlives the app.

Moods are saved as State of Mind and symptoms as real Health samples, so they work with every Health-connected app. Delete Tend tomorrow and your history is still in the Health app, under your control.

Patterns you can actually read.

Tend connects your steps, sleep, active energy, mindful minutes and cycle to how you said you felt, in plain sentences. It shows what tends to move together, and never claims causes.

Symptoms, not just moods.

Headache, fatigue, nausea, brain fog and dozens more, plus your own. Built for migraine, IBS, PMDD, chronic fatigue and anyone whose symptoms do not fit a tidy box.

Privacy

Private by design.

No account, no sign-in, no ads. Your health data stays in Apple Health, on your device, and is never sold or used for tracking. Patterns and AI recaps are worked out on your iPhone.
