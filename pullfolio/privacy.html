Privacy Policy

Your collection stays on your phone.

Last updated 5 October 2026 · Applies to Pullfolio by Ludyem AS.

Your cards, binders, grades, price alerts and price history are stored in the app's own storage on this phone. They're never sent to us or anyone else, and they aren't synced to iCloud.

There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Scanning and photos

Cards are recognised on the phone itself. Camera frames and the photos you choose to scan or grade are read on the phone and aren't uploaded.

Card data and prices

To look a card up and price it, the app asks public card databases for that card: TCGdex, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck, the One Piece TCG API, Lorcast and TCGCSV, and Frankfurter for exchange rates. Those requests name the card, never you, and your collection isn't sent with them. Card pictures load from the same services.

Pictures and sales you choose to share

Where a card has no picture, you can send one of your own, and you can report a sale you made or saw so others can see what a card sells for. Both are optional and only happen when you tap to send. What you send (the picture, or the sale's price, date, marketplace and the link or screenshot you add) goes to the app's public iCloud database, run by Apple. It's checked by us before anyone else sees it, and it's then shown to every Pullfolio user without your name. Sending needs an iCloud account; we don't see who you are.

Alerts and your data

Price alerts are checked and shown by the phone itself. Nothing is sent from a server.

You can export your collection as a CSV file, or delete everything, at any time in Settings.
