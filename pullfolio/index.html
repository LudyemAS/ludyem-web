Features

Scan it. Price it. Keep it.

A trading card scanner and collection tracker, with no subscription and no scan limits.

Scan any card.

Hold a card up and Pullfolio recognises it on your phone and locks on, the likeliest printing first and the others one tap away. Sleeves, top loaders and slabs are fine.

Your collection, priced every day.

TCGplayer and Cardmarket prices in your currency, your collection's value day by day, what moved this week, and an alert for the price you're waiting for.

  • Japanese cardsRead in Japanese, matched by set code and number.
  • A grade before you send itCentering, corners and edges, measured from one photo.
  • A dex for every gameIts own look for each of the five games.

Privacy

Your collection, on your phone.

Your cards, binders and price history stay on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking. Looking a card up asks public card databases for that card, never for you.
