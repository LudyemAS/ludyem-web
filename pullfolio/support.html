Questions
A card isn't found, or the wrong card comes up+

Lay the card flat or hold it still, fill the brackets with it, and tilt it away from lamps so there's no glare. When Pullfolio isn't sure it asks Which one? and shows the likeliest cards; tap the right one. If a card never comes up, write to us with a photo.

Where do the prices come from?+

From TCGplayer and Cardmarket, through public card databases, converted to your currency. Choose the marketplace your cards are valued by in Settings › Prices from. Prices are estimates; what a card sells for depends on its condition and the buyer.

How good is the grade?+

It's an estimate from one photo: the centering is measured from the borders, and the corners and edges are looked at for whitening. The surface can't be judged from a photo, so it isn't. Grading companies may grade differently, and Pullfolio isn't affiliated with any of them.

How do I restore Pullfolio Pro?+

Open Pullfolio Pro and tap Restore purchase. Pro is a single purchase, not a subscription, and is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Where is my collection?+

Only on your phone. There's no account and no iCloud sync. Before you move to a new phone, export it in Settings › Your data › Export collection.

How do I delete everything?+

Settings › Your data › Delete everything.
