StoreGlance

The app that knows the day Apple features you. Everything on this page, including the images, is free to use in coverage.

In one sentence

StoreGlance watches all 175 App Store storefronts every day and sends indie developers one push notification the day their app, or a competitor's, lands on an editorial shelf anywhere in the world.

Why it exists

Apple features thousands of apps a day across 175 storefronts, and tells developers about almost none of it. A developer in Oslo can sit on "Best New Apps" in Japan for a week and find out from a screenshot a stranger posts afterwards. StoreGlance closes that gap: which shelf, which country, which slot, since when, and who is next to you.

What it does

  • Every storefront, every day. All 175 App Store storefronts are checked daily, including the ones a developer would never open.
  • One push the day it happens. A single notification when a tracked app appears on any shelf or Today story, in any storefront. Nothing else, ever.
  • Competitors are first-class. Their shelves are the ones worth knowing about.
  • The shelf, with the neighbours. Each app has its own page: live shelves, the slot, the apps beside it, a 13-week featuring calendar and every earlier placement.
  • Top charts per storefront. The position in each country's charts and how far it moved since yesterday.
  • Browse any store. Open any storefront, see what it is featuring of yours right now, and jump to that country's App Store.
  • A free check for anyone. ludyem.dev/storeglance/check answers "is this app featured right now?" for any app, no account.

Boilerplate

StoreGlance is an iPhone app for developers that watches all 175 App Store storefronts every day and sends one push notification the day an app is featured on an editorial shelf, anywhere in the world. It shows the shelf, the country, the slot and the neighbouring apps, keeps a featuring calendar per app, tracks competitors and top-chart positions per storefront, and needs no account. StoreGlance is made by Ludyem AS in Oslo, the studio behind RCKit and AppGlance, and is available on the App Store with Pro at $0.99 a month.
