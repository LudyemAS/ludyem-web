Featuring

How to know when your app is featured on the App Store

Dylan 2 September 2026 6 min read

Apple tells you about one kind of featuring. If your app lands in select placements on the Today tab, the App Store Connect app sends you a notification. Every other shelf, on every other tab, in every one of the other 170 storefronts, arrives without a word — and usually you hear about it from a stranger on the internet, a week late.

The short answer. App Store Connect covers the marquee Today tab slots. For everything else there is StoreGlance — add your apps once and get a push the day any of the 175 storefronts puts one of them on a shelf, with the shelf name, the country and the slot.

What Apple actually tells you

In November 2024 Apple shipped Featuring Nominations and, alongside it, a notification. The wording in Apple's own announcement is precise, and worth reading twice:

“If you're featured in select placements on the Today tab, you'll also receive a notification via the App Store Connect app.”

Every phrase in that sentence is load-bearing:

  • select placements — a subset, not all of them. In practice the marquee slots: App of the Day, Game of the Day, and the stories Apple's editors treat as headline placements.
  • on the Today tab — not the Apps tab, not the Games tab, not a category page, not a category shelf.
  • via the App Store Connect app — on a phone, with the app installed, notifications allowed, signed in to the account that holds the app.

When it does fire it is genuinely good: tapping the notification hands you a set of Apple-designed “Promote Your App” marketing assets, ready to post. It is simply the top of the pyramid, and the pyramid is much wider than most developers realise.

The part nobody warns you about

The App Store is not one store. It is 175 storefronts, each with its own editors and its own editorial calendar. Every one of them has a Today, an Apps and a Games tab, and every tab is a stack of named shelves — “Best New Apps and Updates”, “Editors' Choice Apps”, “Apps You Might've Missed” and dozens more, each one filled by a person who chose the apps on it.

Apple's notification reaches a slice of one of those tabs. Everything else — the whole Apps tab, the whole Games tab, the category shelves, and all of it repeated across 170 more countries — happens in silence.

Being featured in a country you have never opened the App Store in is the normal case, not the exotic one.

Smaller storefronts feature more aggressively, because there is less to choose from — and they are exactly the ones you will never think to check. “Best New Apps and Updates” exists in Japan too, run by different editors, refreshed on Japanese time, in Japanese. Your App Store Connect app stays quiet.

Option 1: watch App Analytics for a Browse spike

This is where most developers end up, and it is free and official. In App Store Connect, open Analytics → Acquisition → Sources and look at App Store Browse. Apple defines it as:

“Users viewed your app or tapped to download it while browsing the App Store (for example, in the Today, Games, or Apps sections).”

A feature usually shows up as a step change in Browse impressions — not a gentle rise, a cliff. That is a real signal. Its limits are real too:

What it gives you

  • Free, official, no third-party tool
  • Impressions, product page views and downloads, so you can price the feature
  • A territory breakdown, if you go digging

What it doesn't

  • Browse is a bucket: Top Charts, category pages and In-App Events land in it too
  • It never names the shelf you were on
  • It is retrospective — analytics settle over about a day, and shelves rotate
  • Finding the country means reverse-engineering a territory table

So a Browse spike is a hypothesis, not an answer. Working out what caused one is a skill of its own — see your App Store impressions spiked: was it a feature?

Option 2: go looking for it yourself

You can switch your App Store to another country and scroll its editorial tabs. That is fine for a hunch — you launched in Germany, you want to see what Germany is showing today.

It is also the option that quietly fails. There are 175 storefronts, each running to its own calendar and its own clock, and a shelf can appear and rotate away between two of your checks. Doing this thoroughly, every day, in every country, is not a habit anyone keeps for a week — which is why the placements people miss are almost never the ones they went looking for.

What to do the day you find out

A feature is a short window, so the useful moves are the fast ones:

  • Screenshot the shelf while it is live. It disappears when the shelf rotates, and it is the single best piece of marketing collateral you will get for free.
  • Look at the neighbours. The apps beside you on a shelf tell you which editor picked you and what they thought you were.
  • Do not ship that day. A crash spike during your one week on “Best New Apps and Updates” is an expensive way to learn this.
  • Note the date. Editors revisit apps they have featured before, and a featuring history is exactly the thing a nomination can point at.

All four are easier when something hands you the shelf, the country and the neighbours on the morning it happens — which is the whole reason StoreGlance exists.

Frequently asked

Does Apple tell you when your app is featured?+

Only for some placements. Apple's announcement says that if you are featured in select placements on the Today tab, you receive a notification via the App Store Connect app — App of the Day and Game of the Day being the obvious cases. Editorial shelves on the Apps and Games tabs, category shelves, and every placement in the other storefronts arrive with no notification at all. StoreGlance was built to cover exactly that gap.

How can I see if my app is featured in another country?+

There is no report in App Store Connect that lists your placements country by country. StoreGlance covers all 175 storefronts and pushes you the shelf name, the country and the slot on the day it happens.

Can App Store Connect analytics show that I was featured?+

Indirectly. Featuring traffic lands in the App Store Browse acquisition source, but Browse also contains Top Charts, category pages and In-App Events, so a spike is evidence rather than proof — and it arrives after the analytics settle, not on the day.

How long does an App Store feature last?+

It depends on the shelf. App of the Day is one day by definition. Editorial shelves such as “Best New Apps and Updates” typically hold their apps for about a week, and some evergreen collections keep an app far longer. Since a shelf can rotate before you notice, the day it appears is the day worth catching.

Can I see whether a competitor is featured?+

Yes — StoreGlance treats competitors as first-class. Track any app on the store and get the same alert when it lands on a shelf, in any storefront. Knowing which rivals the editors are picking, and on which shelves, is often more useful than watching your own.

How do I get featured in the first place?+

Apple's own route is Featuring Nominations in App Store Connect, with a minimum lead time of three weeks. See the nominations guide.
