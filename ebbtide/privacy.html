Privacy Policy

Your quit data stays on your phone.

Last updated 30 September 2026 · Applies to Ebbtide by Ludyem AS.

Everything you enter stays on this phone: your plan, your logs, your cravings and your reasons. It's stored in the app's own storage and never sent to us or anyone else. It isn't synced to iCloud.

There's no account, no advertising and no tracking.

App usage

From version 1.1, to see how Ebbtide is used, the app sends a few anonymous counts to AppGlance, our own analytics service: when the app is opened and for how long, and steps like finishing setup, opening Ebbtide Pro or buying it. With them go an ID made by the app, the app and iOS versions and your region setting. Never anything about your pouches, cravings or health, and the ID isn't linked to your name or used for tracking. AppGlance keeps the events for at most a year. Version 1.0 sends none of this.

AppGlance's privacy policy

Ebbtide Pro

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on the phone itself; no other company is involved.

Notifications are scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing is sent from a server.

You can export your logs as a CSV file, or delete everything, at any time in Settings.
